# 01 The brief

**Document:** *Website Replacement Architecture & Implementation-Ready Copy*
**Dated:** 8 September 2026
**Origin:** upper management
**Status:** source of truth

## Authority

Stated by the user on 8 Sep 2026: where the brief and the existing site
disagree, **the brief wins**. This explicitly overrides decisions recorded in
the codebase from the earlier Phase A–F design revamp.

That mattered in practice. Several components carried long comments explaining
why something was done a certain way; three of those decisions were reversed by
the brief. In each case the reversal is **recorded in the comment rather than
silently overwriting it** — see [[02 Decisions]].

## What it covers

- A twelve-section homepage narrative, with finished copy for each
- The enquiry form microcopy and field list
- Recommended navigation and footer
- Homepage metadata
- A publication-gate checklist
- A fourteen-item developer handoff

## What it does NOT cover

Worth knowing, because it came up:

- The `/services` and `/industries` **hub pages** are never mentioned. The
  brief refers to "individual service pages" — the eight detail pages — and
  only to say keep them for search intent.
- It says nothing about what the top-level "What We Do" nav item should link
  to. See [[04 Phase 2 — Navigation and footer]].

The brief describes itself as "a copy-and-build brief for the Pixelette
Marketing homepage". Homepage, nav, footer, form, metadata. That is its scope.

## Core repositioning

From a list of marketing activities to a connected growth system built around
commercial outcomes — demand, pipeline, conversion, revenue — **without**
restricting the offer to technology companies.
